# Output Guidance

Use this reference when writing the final review.

## Required Sections

1. Category Ratings
   - Score each relevant category from 0 to 10
   - Give a short rationale for each

2. Overall Rating
   - Give a 0 to 10 score
   - Add one emoji reaction
   - Summarize the overall judgment briefly

3. Checklist Summary
   - Use `✅`, `⚠️`, and `❌`
   - Keep each checklist item short and scannable

4. Suggestions for Improvement
   - Make them specific, concise, and actionable

## Writing Rules

- Findings first, fixes second.
- Do not bury important weaknesses under compliments.
- Keep the review structured: bullet list plus short rationale beats a long
  essay.
- If confidence is limited, say why before over-scoring.
